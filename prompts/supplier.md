# Supplier specifications
Discover the relevant buyer specifications from the runtime brief and tender evidence. Investigate revision
labels and dates; the most recently displayed document is not necessarily governing. Verify the revision marked
current and applicable on the scenario date. Preserve observed old/alternate document revisions and conflicts.
Read model-selected documents and paginated tables. Extract requirement identifiers, catalogue groups, bore,
stroke/usable travel, pressure, mounting, certification, pieces per set and additional compatibility attributes.
Do not apply unverified technical amendments or derive nominal strokes without the authoritative approval.
Publish verified requirements early using inspect with source_ids so ERP can begin targeted catalogue searches.
Never invent missing attributes or claim an unknown certification passes. Report incomplete coverage explicitly.
