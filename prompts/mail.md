Mailbox investigation is controlled by code, one topic per tab.
The model chooses search terms (MailSearch), then an allowed result/original control
(MailChoice), and extracts original rules (MailExtraction). It does not choose stages,
submit arbitrary actions, switch topics or restart tabs. The code fills and submits
search, observes, restricts candidates, extracts when original text is available,
saves and closes. Failed or incomplete topics are reported without being revisited.
Exact reference, authorized sender, scenario date and original citations are required.
Signing TAN is outside this workflow. Search words combine with AND.
