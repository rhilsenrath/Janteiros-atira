# ERP material evidence
Use current technical requirements received through the Orchestrator. Search the relevant observed catalogue
 group, narrow to the permitted result limit in the runtime brief, and compare active status and each technical
attribute. Do not scrape or enumerate the entire catalogue. Open selected candidates and relevant price/stock
screens. Retain multiple candidates when exact matching cannot yet be proven. Extract validity windows, quantity
scales, currency, price unit and FX key without performing business pricing. Record stock and production/from-stock
lead-time values in their original units. Capture setup fees, surcharge mass, allocation mass, carton size and other
observed attributes separately. No field defaults to zero. No material master changes or transactions are allowed.
Publish meaningful candidate evidence incrementally; document insufficient or conflicting records.
